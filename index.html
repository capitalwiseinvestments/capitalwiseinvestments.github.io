FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions from new investors

Can a beginner start investing in mutual funds?

Yes. The appropriate starting point depends on your goals, time horizon, risk tolerance and financial situation.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan allows an investor to invest a chosen amount periodically into a mutual fund scheme.

Do mutual funds offer guaranteed returns?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Returns are not guaranteed.

Can you review my existing mutual fund portfolio?

Yes. A portfolio review can help you understand what you own, possible overlaps and whether the portfolio remains aligned with your objectives.
