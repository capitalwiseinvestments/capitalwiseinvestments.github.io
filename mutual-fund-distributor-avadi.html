QUESTIONS INVESTORS OFTEN ASK

Start with your question.

How much should I invest in a SIP?

There is no universal amount. It depends on your cash flow, goals, time horizon and risk capacity.

Should I invest a lump sum all at once?

The answer depends on the source of the money, investment objective, time horizon and how you are comfortable managing market risk.

Can you review mutual funds I already hold?

Yes. A portfolio review can help you understand what you own, potential overlaps and whether the investments remain aligned with your objectives.

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks and returns are not guaranteed.
